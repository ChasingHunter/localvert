# ADR-NNNN: Short title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [ADR-NNNN](NNNN-….md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forced a decision. The constraint, the conflict, or the thing that broke.
Facts and numbers, not narrative. A reader in two years should understand the
pressure without having been here.

## Decision

What we are doing, in the imperative. One paragraph is usually enough.

## Consequences

What this buys and what it costs. Be honest about the cost — an ADR that lists
only benefits is a sales pitch and will not help the person who has to revisit
it.

## Alternatives considered

Each option with the reason it lost. "We did not think of it" is a valid entry
if it is true and found out later.
